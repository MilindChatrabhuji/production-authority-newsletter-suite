# Pack 1: Core Newsletter Authority & Sponsorship Engine
**Way #67 / Product 69 (Category D: Content, Media & Audience)**

Provides industrial-grade curation synthesis, sponsor ROI metrics tracking, automated media kit rate cards, subscriber churn detection, and multi-platform RSS syndication.

## Features
- **Multi-Source Link Curation**: Sectioned newsletter synthesis with verified scientific/software evidence citations.
- **Sponsor Metrics & ROI Tracker**: Real-time click tracking, CPM/CPC calculator, and executive PDF/Markdown sponsor reporting.
- **Dynamic Media Kit Generator**: Automated subscriber tiers, open rate benchmarking, and commercial rate cards ($300 - $2,500/mo).
- **Subscriber Churn Guard**: Detects unopened issue patterns, delivers re-engagement winback sequences, and protects sender deliverability.
- **Cross-Platform Syndicator**: Instant formatting for beehiiv, Substack, LinkedIn Newsletters, and Web RSS 2.0.
